# Community Support Hub

Welcome to the public community support forum for [Prestomedia](https://prestomedia.com) applications! 

This space is dedicated to user-to-user discussions, feature requests, and general Q&A. Please note that *this is a public community forum*.



## 📱 Select Your Application

To ask a question, report an issue, or suggest an idea, click on your app's discussion category below:

* 📝 [**Free Text Editor**](https://github.com/PrestomediaLLC/community-en/discussions/categories/free-text-editor-com)
* 📊 [**Pro Diagrams**](https://github.com/PrestomediaLLC/community-en/discussions/categories/prodiagrams-com) (coming soon)
* 📊 [**Free Diagrams**](https://github.com/PrestomediaLLC/community-en/discussions/categories/freediagrams-com) (coming soon)
* 📊 [**Team Matcher**](https://github.com/PrestomediaLLC/community-en/discussions/categories/team-matcher-com) (coming soon)

*(When you click **"New discussion"** inside a category, a pre-formatted form will guide you through posting your message.)*



## 📋 Community Guidelines

Before posting, please keep the following in mind:

1. **Search First:** Check existing discussions in your app's category—your question or feature request may already be answered.
2. **Protect Your Privacy:** **Never post private personal information**, email addresses, passwords, license keys, or sensitive financial data. All posts are completely public.
3. **Be Respectful:** Treat fellow community members with kindness. Friendly, constructive discussions help us build better apps for everyone!
